The changelog

What shipped and when. This site preaches building small focused software in public — the least we can do is keep a public record of our own.

October 24, 2026

  • Fourth guide: How to build a Typeform alternative. One question at a time, a response table, and a formula-safe CSV, with prompts that keep the agent off the visual builder.

October 23, 2026

  • A guides section: step-by-step tutorials that walk one catalog app from stack and data model through prompts, checks, failures, and deploy. Index at /guides/, RSS, sitemap, and Open Graph cards next to the blog.
  • First guide: Vibe code a Calendly clone. Weekly hours, a booking page, and a lock so two guests cannot take the same slot.
  • Second guide: Build your own link shortener step by step. Slugs, redirects, and a click count, with prompts that keep the agent off Bitly's week-long row.
  • Third guide: How to build a Linktree alternative. A profile, five links, one theme, and a publish snapshot, with prompts that keep the agent off the theme marketplace.

October 19, 2026

  • Rewrote all 109 build-guide titles around searches like "how to build your own with ezvibecode Slack alternative," with matching headings, social titles, and article metadata.
  • Added 30 apps and services, bringing the catalog from 79 to 109. New builds cover inventory, client onboarding, API docs, hiring, class bookings, maintenance, daily planning, journaling, and habits.
  • Every addition has three scope estimates, complete build prompts in four frameworks, and four smaller feature prompts. That adds 120 mini-prompts, bringing the total to 436.
  • New guides link to official pricing pages and explain billing periods, seat costs, and excluded extras. Prices were checked on October 19, 2026.

October 9, 2026

  • A blog: one article a day about which subscriptions are worth replacing, starting with why you should not rebuild Notion first. RSS, a sitemap, product cross-links, the usual SEO.

August 3, 2026

  • Build just one piece: every product page now has standalone mini-prompts for the peculiar sub-problems hiding inside each product — 316 of them, four per product. The debt simplifier inside Splitwise, the DST-safe slot math inside Calendly, the backlink index inside Notion.
  • Same build, different logo: 68 products now list the competitors the same v1 replaces — build the Calendly replacement, cancel SavvyCal and Cal.com too.
  • Every product got its own page: 79 full build guides with the complete prompt, honest scope, FAQs, structured data, a sitemap, and proper canonicals. Search "how to build your own X" and hopefully find us.
  • Honest three-tier estimates on every card, because "Notion in 10 hours" was a lie. Now each product shows what a basic version, a solid v1, and the full honest rebuild actually take.
  • The catalog more than doubled in one day: 44 new products (35 → 79), including Figma, Photoshop, Grammarly, Shopify, IFTTT, Splitwise, Gumroad, Roam Research, Sentry, and Ghost.

July 31, 2026

  • Framework tabs: every build prompt now ships in four flavors — the AHA stack (Astro, HTMX, Alpine.js), Next.js, Laravel, and Ruby on Rails. Pick the one you already know.
  • Added YNAB and Toggl Track to the catalog (33 → 35).

July 30, 2026 — Launch

  • ezvibecode goes live with 33 subscriptions, each with real pricing, a build-it-yourself verdict, and a complete scoped v1 prompt to hand your coding agent.
  • Team-aware pricing from day one, because "per person per month" is how they get you.
  • A suggestion form, a custom favicon, and a social card. The essentials.
